The problem

Sound familiar?

LCAMs manage dozens of properties, hundreds of homeowners, and thousands of hours — tracked in spreadsheets, notebooks, and memory.

📊

Hours tracked in spreadsheets

You're manually logging start and end times in Excel, trying to reconcile them at the end of the week against contract limits.

📞

Phone calls go untracked

You spend hours each week returning homeowner calls but struggle to capture and prove that time against your contract.

⚠️

Violations tracked on paper

Violation notices are written by hand or in Word docs, with no easy way to track escalation status across multiple properties.

🏢

No visibility for leadership

Regional Directors and VPs have no real-time view of how hours are being used across their portfolio of properties.
